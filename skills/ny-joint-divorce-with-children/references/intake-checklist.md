# Intake checklist (ask everything in one pass)

Say once: you are transcribing what they give you, not giving legal advice,
and an attorney should review before filing. Do **not** ask for Social
Security numbers; they are handwritten later.

## Case
- County of filing; index number (if bought); date summons filed / served
- Who files the Note of Issue (plaintiff, plaintiff's attorney, defendant)
- Which residency rule applies (2 years; 1 year + married or lived in NY; 1 year + breakdown in NY; both live in NY)
- Is either spouse in the military?

## Each spouse
- Full legal name, birth name, any prior names (up to 3)
- Current address (street, city, state, zip); past addresses in the last 3 years
- Date of birth, state of birth (country only if born abroad), sex (optional)
- County and locality (city/town/village; inside city or village limits?)
- Phone; on public assistance?
- Represented? Attorney name, address, phone, fax
- Group health plan: name, address, ID number, administrator (or none)
- Wants to resume a former last name? Which one?
- DOH-2168: marriage number (first, second...), how prior marriages ended, years of schooling (0-17)
- Last year's income: gross; NYC/Yonkers tax; Social Security; Medicare; maintenance paid to a different spouse; child support paid for other children

## Marriage
- Date and place (city, county, state, country); civil or religious ceremony
- Religious: have barriers to remarriage been removed, or is that waived?
- Month and year the couple separated

## Children (every child under 21)
- Name, date of birth, sex, address, who they live with, relationship of that person
- Any child on public assistance?
- Under 18: legal custody and physical custody (joint / spouse 1 / spouse 2) for each child
- Existing Family Court custody order (county, docket, date) or custody agreement (date)?
- Parenting time: agreement attached, or the written schedule (and which spouse visits)
- Number of children ever born alive of this marriage

## Child support
- Which parent is the custodial parent for support purposes?
- Support Collection Unit: who, and requesting / already applied / already receives / does not want?
- Existing Family Court support order to continue? (payor, amount, frequency, county, docket, date)
- Otherwise: separate agreement (date) or agreed in this form?
- Paid to spouse 1, spouse 2, another person, or through the SCU? Weekly, every two weeks, monthly?
- If combined income is over $193,000: apply the percentage to the excess or not?
- Do they want a different amount than the guideline? If yes, the amount and the reason
- Child care: each parent's % and amount and frequency
- Education / extraordinary expenses: what, who pays, amount or %
- Health insurance: covered by state plan / spouse 1 / spouse 2; premium and frequency; each parent's %; uninsured costs split; QMCSO wanted?
- Income deduction order: issue, alternative arrangement, or good cause (why)?

Run the CSSA worksheet and get an explicit OK on the figures before filling.

## Background check (children under 18)
- Any order of protection ever (against whom, county, court, docket)?
- Any child abuse/neglect proceeding (docket and county)?
- Is either spouse on the sex offender registry?

## Maintenance
- None, or agreed (separate written agreement or in this form)?
- Payor, amount, frequency, start and end (or duration), how paid
- Optional: guideline amount and advisory duration from the court calculator

## Property and fees
- Marital home: who gets exclusive occupancy, address, until when / on what terms
- Property: already divided / separate agreement / terms written into the form
- Real estate transfer: who to whom, address, deadline
- Pensions: QDRO / separate agreement / both waive
- Debts: none / separate agreement / terms written into the form
- Lawyer's fees: each pays own, or who pays how much; experts' fees
- Settlement agreement date, if there is one
