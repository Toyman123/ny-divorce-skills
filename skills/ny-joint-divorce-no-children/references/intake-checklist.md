# Intake checklist

Ask for all of this in one message. Do not ask for Social Security numbers.

## Both spouses
1. Full legal name (first, middle, last) and birth name if different
2. Full street address, city, state, zip
3. Residence county, and whether the address is inside city/village limits (if not, the town)
4. Date of birth and **state** of birth (state, not "USA")
5. Sex marker (optional field on DOH-2168)
6. Phone
7. Represented by an attorney? If yes: name, firm address, phone, fax
8. On public assistance? (yes/no)
9. Race, number of this marriage (first/second/etc.), how any prior marriages ended (death / divorce / none), highest grade completed

## Case
10. County where filing
11. Index number and date index issued, if already assigned
12. Date summons filed / served, if applicable
13. Who files the Note of Issue

## Marriage
14. Date of marriage; city/town, county, state, country
15. Civil or religious ceremony (if religious: barriers to remarriage removed, or waived)
16. Approximate month/year the couple separated
17. Residency basis - one of:
    - one spouse in NY 2+ years
    - one spouse in NY 1+ year AND married in NY or lived in NY as a couple
    - one spouse in NY 1+ year AND breakdown happened in NY
    - both live in NY AND breakdown happened in NY
18. Either spouse in the military?

## Terms
19. Health insurance - plan name, address, ID/plan number, administrator, for each
20. Maintenance - none, or amount/frequency/duration
21. Marital property - already divided / separate written agreement / write-in language
22. Marital home exclusive occupancy - N/A or details
23. Real estate transfer - N/A or address and deadline
24. Pensions and retirement - QDRO / separate agreement / mutual waiver
25. **Debt and liabilities** - N/A / separate agreement / write-in (easy to forget)
26. Former last name for either spouse
27. Lawyer and expert fees - each pays own, or who pays what
28. Who serves the Judgment with Notice of Entry
29. Separate Settlement Agreement? If yes, its date

## JD-5 income table (page 4) - last year, for each spouse
30. Gross total income before taxes
31. NYC or Yonkers taxes paid
32. FICA Social Security taxes paid
33. Medicare taxes paid
34. Court-ordered maintenance paid to a different spouse
35. Court-ordered child support for children not of this marriage

## DOH-2168
36. Number of children ever born alive of this marriage; number under 18 in the family
37. Decree granted to (usually the plaintiff)
38. Who is preparing the certificate
